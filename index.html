03 / Wheebuy shopping guide

A shortlist you
can actually use.

A Wheebuy finds list becomes more useful when every entry answers the same practical questions. Keep enough information to compare products without reopening each page from scratch.

Start browsing on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose one category first

    Start with sneakers, a layer or an accessory. Compare items with the same intended use before mixing unrelated products into one list.

  2. Save the original product reference

    Record the source URL, selected color, size and date checked. A category page helps discovery; the original listing supplies the order details.

  3. Check fit and visual evidence

    Compare measurements with something you own. Look for clear views of construction, labels, closures and the exact variant you intend to buy.

  4. Confirm compatibility and total cost

    Check whether the agent supports the source and item. Review fees, delivery estimates, restrictions and return conditions before paying.

05 / Read the full number

The listed price is one part of the budget.

Estimated purchase total Item price + domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties

Record the currency and the date of each quote. Shipping estimates can depend on destination, package dimensions, weight and the selected delivery service. Confirm the final quote before payment.

06 / Read the actual item

QC photos help with visible details.

When reviewing quality-control photos, compare the selected size and color with the order. Look for visible damage, uneven seams, missing components and mismatched measurements.

Photos have limits: they cannot establish long-term durability, hidden material composition or authenticity by themselves. Ask for missing information instead of treating an attractive image as complete evidence.

07 / Clear answers

Before you
follow a link.

Quick answers for spreadsheet browsing, sizing and purchase planning.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

In shopping searches, a Wheebuy spreadsheet usually means a table of product references that shoppers want to review for use with Wheebuy. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Wheebuy spreadsheet or downloadable inventory.

Where do the category links on this page go?

All category links open the corresponding CAFANS category. General browsing links lead to www.cafans.com. A category link does not confirm that a pictured item is available.

Can every CAFANS listing be purchased through Wheebuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for every listing. Check the original product URL, supported marketplace, item restrictions and current agent terms before placing an order.

How should I compare sneaker and clothing sizes?

For sneakers, compare the stated size system and foot-length guidance. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own. Do not assume the same label size fits equally across different listings.

Do brand photos prove authenticity or availability?

No. Editorial reference photos do not authenticate a seller’s goods or confirm inventory. Review the actual listing, seller information, provenance and applicable return terms.

How do I estimate the full purchase cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Check the currency and quote date. The final total depends on the order and destination.
